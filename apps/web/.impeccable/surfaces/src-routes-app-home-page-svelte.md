# Surface brief — the tutor app (apps/web)

Mode: **Operate** (a working product of its category; the world lends only type,
palette, density, and one signature move). Primary target:
`apps/web/src/routes/(app)/home/+page.svelte`; the world applies to every route.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** The app is a page of a night dictionary, not a dashboard. It refuses
the card-grid-plus-KPI-row arrangement every language app ships: values are ruled
entries, and the language itself is printed on paper. One light surface, one
seal-red action, brass figures for what you earned.

**OWN-WORLD.** Deep ink ground (`#090d12` → `#1a242f`), a single paper plate
(`#efe6d3`), vermilion **seal** (`#e2452b`) for the one forward action, **jade**
(`#45c08a`) for what grows, **brass gold** (`#eac168`) for earned numbers, and
azure / violet / brass / ember as semantic data colours that always mean the same
thing. Book serif (Iowan / Songti) for characters and figures, the system sans for
UI text. 3–5px radii, 1px rules, no shadows, no gradients.

**STORY.** A member on the couch opens the app and reads their rhythm at a
glance: a heat map of the last weeks of practice, then the numbers they have
earned — in that order.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** Eyebrow (`Mandarin · HSK 1`), serif handle, vermilion level
seal; a jade **activity heat map** panel (18 Monday-first weeks with month
labels and a less→more legend); ruled entries below for XP earned, day streak, a
jade daily-goal bar and an azure next-level bar.

**FORM.** Candidate 7 of 7 grounded directions (night dictionary / manuscript
lexicon). seed:4c820cebc24533d3

**FINISH.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance.
