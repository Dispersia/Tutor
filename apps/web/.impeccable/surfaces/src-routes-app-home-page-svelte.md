# Surface brief — the tutor app (apps/web)

Mode: **Operate** (a working product of its category; the world lends only type,
palette, density, and one signature move). Primary target:
`apps/web/src/routes/(app)/home/+page.svelte`; the world applies to every route.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** The app is the ledger of a game you actually open every day, not a
dashboard. It refuses the card-grid-plus-KPI-row arrangement every language app
ships: values are ruled entries, the language is printed on a ruled cell, and the
chrome stays quiet so the practice leads. One amber action, brass figures for
what you earned, four muted hues for the professions.

**OWN-WORLD.** Neutral graphite ground (`oklch(0.16 0.006 258)` →
`oklch(0.955 0.004 258)`), **Seal Amber** (`oklch(0.7 0.145 52)`) for the one
forward action, **jade** (`oklch(0.73 0.1 158)`) for what grows, **brass gold**
(`oklch(0.8 0.105 88)`) for earned numbers, and azure / violet / brass / ember as
semantic data colours that always mean the same thing. Space Grotesk for display,
with a native CJK serif behind it; Rubik for body, with a native CJK sans. 10–14px
radii, 1px hairlines, one soft shadow, no gradients, no warm cast.

**STORY.** A member on the couch opens the app and reads their rhythm at a
glance: a heat map of the last weeks of practice, then the numbers they have
earned — in that order.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** The handle in the display face, a graphite eyebrow
(`Mandarin · HSK 1`), an amber level seal; a jade **activity heat map** panel
(Monday-first weeks with month labels and a less→more legend); ruled entries
below for XP earned, day streak, a jade daily-goal bar and an azure next-level
bar; then the idle **rigs** panel and the bank strip.

**FORM.** Flat graphite surfaces, generous radii (14px panels, 12px tiles, 10px
buttons, pill chips and bars), 1px hairlines, a single 2px-offset focus ring in
Seal Amber. The signature move is the ruled character cell.

**FINISH.** Unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished: this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, `DESIGN.md`, and every shipping font carrying its
provenance.
